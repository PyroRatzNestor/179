Branch 179 – Irrigation System Winterization (Back-Drain)

4 submersible well pumps · stick-built pump houses · mild-day use expected
0% complete
Purpose: Back-drain (relieve pressure from) the irrigation system before extended below-freezing temperatures to prevent freeze damage to: automatic filters, manual filters, above-ground pump-house piping, the 3 in. hydrometer and connecting pipes, in-ground electric valves, quick-connect hose connections, and the well/city water split air-gap pipes and fittings.
Heat caution: Pump houses are insulated with heaters on all winter, but the heaters are not intended to run 24/7 in extended freezing weather. A long power loss in freezing temperatures can cause serious damage – drain the system; do not rely on heaters.
Added notes (not on original sheets – verify): Submersible pumps sit below the frost line in the wells, so protection focuses on above-ground pump-house piping, filters, spigots, and shallow valve boxes. Because the system may run on mild winter days, a Mild-Day Use and Re-Drain section is included at the end. Blue ADDED items are suggestions, not part of the original procedure.

Planning guide (ADDED): Have the system winterized by Oct 20, before the earliest typical first freeze. Treat any forecast low of 32°F or below as a re-drain trigger.
